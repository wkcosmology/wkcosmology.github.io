Welcome to Kosmos!

Hello! I am Kai Wang, a Chinese astrophysicist studying the formation and evolution of galaxies, and how it is related to the large-scale structure in our own real Universe and numerically simulated universes. I’ve always had a mind that jumps quickly between ideas, but for me it has been a source of curiosity, creativity, and momentum. It pushes me to follow unexpected connections, to ask unusual questions, and to build research projects across disciplines and techniques.
